SMS Gateway

This deployment is running in its operator's own Cloudflare account.

You are looking at the API host, not the console.
  • Device traffic is ECDSA-signed and lives under /v1/device/* and /v1/sms/*.
  • Operator endpoints live under /v1/admin/* and require a bearer token.
  • Health and installed version: GET /v1/admin/health (admin token required).
